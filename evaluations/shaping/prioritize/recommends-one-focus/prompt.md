I'm building a booking app for a small climbing gym and I keep going around in circles. Ideas: members book a slot in a session, staff manage session capacity, monthly memberships with online payment, a waiver signed at first visit, a chat between members and coaches, and an attendance dashboard for the owner. Users are members, front-desk staff, and the owner. Constraints: one developer, the gym opens bookings in six weeks, payments must go through the gym's existing Stripe account. Out of scope: a native mobile app. Which of these should I build first, and what can wait?

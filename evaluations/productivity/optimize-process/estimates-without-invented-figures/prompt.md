Every Friday I produce a customer-support report. A script exports the week's tickets to a spreadsheet (about 10 minutes, observed). I then read through the tickets and tag themes by hand, which takes me around two hours. I send the draft to my manager, who usually replies the next Monday. After corrections I publish it on Tuesday. I want to have an AI agent do the theme tagging. How much would that save me?

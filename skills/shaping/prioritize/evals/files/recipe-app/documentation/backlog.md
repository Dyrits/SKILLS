# Backlog

## Direction

Plumb is a recipe-sharing app for home cooks. The next release should help a cook go from "what shall I make" to "I have what I need" without leaving the app. Constraint: the app must keep working on a spotty kitchen connection.

## Outcomes

### Cooks can plan a week of meals

Intended result: a cook drags recipes onto days of a week.
Why it matters: it is the most requested capability in user interviews.
Status: approved for later.

### Cooks can build a shopping list from a plan

Intended result: ingredients from planned recipes merge into one list.
Why it matters: it turns a plan into action.
Prerequisite (suspected): ingredient names must be normalised ("tomatoes" and "tomato").
Status: candidate.

### Cooks can share a shopping list with a household

Intended result: two people tick items off the same list.
Prerequisite (confirmed): needs accounts and shared storage.
Status: candidate.

### Cooks can use the app offline

Intended result: saved recipes and the current list work without a connection.
Why it matters: kitchens have poor signal.
Status: candidate.

### Cooks can follow a social feed

Intended result: a feed of recipes from followed cooks.
Status: candidate.

## Out of scope

- Grocery delivery integration: too much partner work for this release.

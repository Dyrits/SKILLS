# Mission: Sourdough baking

## Why
Bake one reliable sourdough loaf each Sunday for the family, without buying bread, starting with a starter I already keep alive.

## Success looks like
- Feed and judge the readiness of a starter by sight and smell
- Produce a loaf with an open crumb and a crisp crust at home, in a regular oven

## Constraints
- A home oven only, no Dutch oven yet
- One bake per week

## Out of scope
- Selling bread
- Pastry and enriched doughs

# Mission: Reading sheet music

## Why
Play the right-hand melody of three simple folk songs on a keyboard at my niece's birthday party in March, reading from the score instead of memorising by ear.

## Success looks like
- Name any note on the treble clef staff within about two seconds
- Play a four-bar melody in C major from the score at a slow steady pace
- Follow quarter, half, and whole note durations

## Constraints
- Twenty minutes a day, on a 61-key keyboard
- Prefers short lessons and printable cheat sheets

## Out of scope
- The bass clef and left-hand chords
- Music theory beyond what is needed to read the three songs

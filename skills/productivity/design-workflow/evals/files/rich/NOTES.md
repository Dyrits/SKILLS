# Notes on my world

## Tools
- Mail: a shared inbox (`billing@fernhill.example`) where suppliers send invoices as PDF attachments.
- Accounting: LedgerLeaf, which has an import API and a "draft bill" state.
- Chat: Teamline, one channel `#finance` where Ines (the owner) answers questions.
- Storage: a shared folder `Invoices/YYYY-MM/`.

## Channels I process
- Supplier invoices (about 40 a week, mostly PDF, a few photos of paper invoices).
- Credit notes (rare, about 2 a month).

## My terms
- "to book it" means create the draft bill in LedgerLeaf.
- "to clear it" means Ines approved the draft and it is posted.
- "odd one" means an invoice from a supplier we have never seen.

## Constraints
- Anything over 2,000 euros must be cleared by Ines personally.
- I am in the office Monday to Thursday, mornings only.

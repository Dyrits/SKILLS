# Expense approval at Quillmark (current)

1. Employee photographs the receipt and emails it to the finance mailbox.
2. Finance assistant retypes the amount, date, and vendor into the accounting tool (about 6 minutes per receipt, about 30 receipts a week).
3. Manager approves by replying "ok" to a forwarded email (usually same day, sometimes 3 days).
4. Any expense over 5,000 euros also needs the finance director's signature. The external auditor requires this control and samples it every year.
5. Finance assistant pays the employee on the Friday run.

Pain reported: employees wait up to two weeks for reimbursement; the finance assistant spends a day a week on retyping.

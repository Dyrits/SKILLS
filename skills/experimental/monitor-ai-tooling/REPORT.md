# Report frame

Use this structure, keeping missing evidence visible.

```markdown
# AI tooling report: <project>, <period>

## Observations

<Observed benefits, quality findings, and unavailable measurements.>

| Tool | Client / scope | Usage evidence | Reduction or timing evidence | Quality evidence | Gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <Every configured tool> | <Project or global> | <Source and count, or unknown> | <Value, unit, and method, or unknown> | <Checked outcome or missing check> | <Missing attribution, baseline, or coverage> |

## Sources and calculations

<Source paths or commands, timestamps, schema/version, scope, resets, and reproducible arithmetic.>
<Separate actual usage, output reduction, estimates, task comparisons, and external benchmarks.>

## Next actions

<Evidence-supported configuration recommendations and specific missing measurements.>
<Recovery gaps, including whether context compaction and allowance exhaustion were separately checked.>
```

A sparse report is useful when it identifies exactly what has not been measured.
Keep sensitive supporting artifacts local; link to them without copying credentials or personal transcripts into the report.

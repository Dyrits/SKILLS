# Introducing Fernwood 2.4: A Game-Changing Update For Plant Sitters

Fernwood 2.4 stands as a pivotal milestone in our journey, showcasing our commitment to a vibrant and thriving community of plant lovers. Experts believe that consistent watering is crucial for healthy plants, and this release underscores that insight, fostering better habits while enhancing the overall experience.

## Key Highlights

- **Reminders:** Reminders now let you set a weekly watering schedule.
- **Photos:** Photos can be attached to each plant, ensuring you never lose track of how it looks.
- **Sharing:** Sharing lets a neighbor see your plants, from beginners to seasoned experts to everyone in between.

In order to get started, it is important to note that you must update to version 2.4. Due to the fact that the old reminder format was removed, existing reminders are migrated automatically on first launch, which takes about two seconds per 100 plants.

Not just a bug fix release, but a true leap forward, Fernwood 2.4 leverages a robust, intuitive, and seamless approach to plant care. The future looks bright! I hope this helps, and let me know if you have any questions.
